Formz is a phone-first editor for Google Forms. Sign in with your Google account to create forms, edit questions, publish and share links, and read responses. Everything is saved to Google Forms in your own Google account.

## How Formz uses your Google data
Formz uses Google's APIs only to work with your own forms on your behalf. It asks for these permissions:

- **Google Drive files you use with Formz** (`drive.file`): list, search, and rename the forms you create in Formz, pick with Google's file picker, or open by link. Formz can't see the rest of your Drive.
- **Your Google Forms** (`forms.body`): open, create, edit, and publish the forms you work on.
- **Responses to your Google Forms** (`forms.responses.readonly`): show responses as charts and individual answers.
- **Your name, email address, and profile photo**: sign you in and show which Google account is connected.

Formz is a form editor. It does not use AI or machine learning, does not generate images, video, or other media, and does not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train AI or machine-learning models. It does not use Google user data for advertising and does not sell it. Formz's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Build forms on your phone
Add and edit questions: short and long answers, multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdowns, linear scales, ratings, grids, dates, and times. Mark questions required, remove them, and change the form title and description. Edits stay on your phone until you tap Save, then go straight to Google Forms.

## Bring your existing forms
Choose forms from Google Drive with Google's file picker, or paste a form's editor link. Formz only sees the files you create in it, pick, or open; it can't browse the rest of your Drive.

## Share and collect responses
Publish a form and share its link from your phone. See responses as charts and as individual answers.

## Try it first
Explore the demo without signing in. Demo changes stay on your phone and aren't saved.

## Privacy
Formz has no ads or cross-app tracking. Your forms and responses stay in Google Forms; Formz's server keeps only what it needs to connect to Google on your behalf, and its usage analytics never include your form content. Delete your account any time in Settings. Read the [privacy policy](/apps/formz/privacy) for details.
