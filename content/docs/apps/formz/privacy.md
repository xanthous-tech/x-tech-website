Effective October 4, 2026. Formz for iPhone and Android is provided by Xanthous Tech LLC ("we"). This policy explains what Formz collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## How Formz works

Formz is an editor for Google Forms. When you sign in with Google, Formz's server uses your permission to call Google's APIs on your behalf: to create and edit your forms, publish them, and read their responses. Your forms, responses, and Drive files stay in your Google account.

## Information from your Google account

When you sign in, we receive and store your Google account ID, name, email address, and profile photo address. We also store the access and refresh tokens Google issues, encrypted, so Formz can keep working with your forms until you sign out or delete your account.

Formz asks for these Google permissions:

- **See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with Formz** (`drive.file`): to list, search, and rename forms you create in Formz, pick with Google's file picker, or open in Formz. Formz can't see the rest of your Drive.
- **See, edit, create, and delete your Google Forms forms** (`forms.body`): to open, create, edit, and publish the forms you work on.
- **See all responses to your Google Forms forms** (`forms.responses.readonly`): to show responses in the app.

## Forms and responses

Form content and responses are retrieved from Google when you open them and sent to your phone. Our server does not keep copies of them. When you open a form from a link, we store its ID and title so it stays in your form list. Unsaved edits are kept on your phone only until you save or leave the form.

## Other information we process

To keep you signed in, our server stores a session with its creation and expiry times, IP address, and device type (user agent). Our hosting provider records operational logs of requests, such as IP address, time, and address requested, to run and secure the service. On your phone, Formz keeps its sign-in session in the system's secure storage and remembers whether you have seen its tips.

## Usage analytics

Formz uses PostHog to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It records app events (such as opening the app, screens viewed, which buttons are used, and actions like creating or publishing a form, with counts and error codes), along with your Formz account ID and device and app details such as model, operating system, app version, and language. It never records your email address, form titles, questions, or responses. Analytics data is linked to your Formz account ID, kept for up to one year, and deleted with your account on request.

Formz has no advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Formz's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide Formz's features to you. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. People at Xanthous Tech LLC do not read your Google data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

## Service providers

Formz's server and database run on Cloudflare. Usage analytics are processed by PostHog. Sign-in and form features are provided by Google's APIs, whose use of your information is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We share information with these providers only to operate Formz.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your account information, tokens, and saved form links while your Formz account exists. Signing out ends the session on your phone. Expired sessions are removed.

Delete your account in **Settings → Delete account**. This deletes your Formz account, sessions, stored Google tokens, and saved form links, and asks Google to revoke Formz's access. It does not delete forms, responses, or files in your Google account. If you can't use the app, email [formz@x-tech.io](mailto:formz@x-tech.io) from your account's email address and we will delete your account. You can also remove Formz's access at any time in your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Hosting logs and backups expire on their providers' schedules.

## Support

When you email [formz@x-tech.io](mailto:formz@x-tech.io), we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to legal retention requirements.

## Children

Formz is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Contact and changes

Contact Xanthous Tech LLC at [formz@x-tech.io](mailto:formz@x-tech.io) with privacy questions or requests. We will update this policy and its effective date if these practices change.
