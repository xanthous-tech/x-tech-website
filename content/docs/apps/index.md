## Ring Sizer

Estimate a ring or finger size from a photo and a standard card. Review the detected edges, compare international sizes, and keep your measurements on your iPhone.

Free for iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

[About Ring Sizer](/apps/ringsizer) · [Support & FAQ](/apps/ringsizer/support) · [Privacy policy](/apps/ringsizer/privacy)

## SubPorter

Move Stripe subscriptions between accounts during a business sale, acquisition, or move to a new legal entity. SubPorter guides account owners through planning, copying customers, and recreating subscriptions around their renewal dates.

[Visit SubPorter](https://subporter.com)

## MapAnimate

Create animated flight-map videos from your travel itinerary. Choose airports, adjust the route, timing, camera, and labels, then export a video in landscape, vertical, or square format.

[Visit MapAnimate](https://mapanimate.app)
