## Ring Sizer

<img src="/apps/ringsizer/icon.png" alt="Ring Sizer app icon" width={112} height={112} />

Estimate a ring or finger size from a photo and a standard card. Review the detected edges, compare international sizes, and keep your measurements on your phone.

Free on Google Play for Android 9 or later. Coming soon for iPhone.
